## The construction theme — `webui/construction.css` (dev, not on prod)

WHAT CHANGED. One stylesheet, scoped to `html[data-product="construction"]`
(set statically in CET's index.html and again by `applyProduct`), loaded
after tokens.css and style.css. It overrides the tokens and restyles a
short list of components; the shared engine's forest-green system is
untouched for the excavation product. Thesis: the plan room - the white
sheet is the brightest thing, the table is dark graphite with a drafting
grid, everything else reads like a drawing's title block (mono caps section
labels numbered 01, 02..., ruled cards, radii 3/4/6 instead of 4/8/12), one
accent (safety orange) for the next action only, verdicts as sign colours
(go green, caution YELLOW - moved off amber so it never reads as the
accent, stop red), and a hatch stripe on the masthead as the single
authored moment. Paper theme: warm drafting paper on a concrete table with
the accent darkened for contrast. IBM Plex stays (self-hosted), the 12px
floor stays, mono = data stays.

WHAT THE LIVE PASS FOUND. The first render stacked the brand's sub-line and
the Projects / Settings links into a column (a flex column on `.brand`);
fixed by leaving style.css's inline layout alone. The flooring table's
`$/LF base` header wrapped under mono caps; shortened to `$/LF`.
