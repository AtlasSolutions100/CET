## CET's own look — "the plan room" (dev, not on prod)

WHAT CHANGED. The first cut (b8f196b) was one stylesheet scoped to a
product attribute so the engine's green system survived underneath; Koby
ruled that out the same day - the apps are independent, nothing is shared,
duplicate what is needed. So the palette now IS CET's `webui/tokens.css`,
the component rules are the last section of CET's `webui/style.css`, the
scoped file and the attribute are gone, the brand is written into
index.html as CET, and the login, change-password, terms and privacy pages,
the favicon (SVG and PIL-drawn rasters) and the lockup are CET's own,
recoloured and renamed. Thesis: the plan room - the white
sheet is the brightest thing, the table is dark graphite with a drafting
grid, everything else reads like a drawing's title block (mono caps section
labels numbered 01, 02..., ruled cards, radii 3/4/6 instead of 4/8/12), one
accent (safety orange) for the next action only, verdicts as sign colours
(go green, caution YELLOW - moved off amber so it never reads as the
accent, stop red), and a hatch stripe on the masthead as the single
authored moment. Paper theme: warm drafting paper on a concrete table with
the accent darkened for contrast. IBM Plex stays (self-hosted), the 12px
floor stays, mono = data stays.

WHAT THE LIVE PASS FOUND (every surface, both themes; the table is in

## What the flooring stops at — fixtures inside a room

Koby's recording (capture (21).webm) posed it: clicking a room with every
line showing gives a fill cut by door swings, tubs, cabinets and beds;
walls-only gives the whole room; which does a flooring takeoff want?
Answer: the whole room, less the fixtures flooring stops at. Built:
`rooms.room_objects` lists the CELLS the non-wall linework encloses
inside the fill (a tub is rarely one closed loop - Seer Stone draws it as
a rim against three walls plus an inner outline; a vanity is an open run
of cabinet lines), names each by the layer owning most of its boundary
plus its size, drops a cell holding a detected door-swing midpoint, and
merges nested cells of one fixture. Defaults: tubs and showers OUT,
cabinets IN (Koby: "not cabinets"), everything else in. The panel: a
per-kind default row, a per-room object list with a tick each, the room
line as `53 SF − tub 9.2 SF = 44 SF`, hatched cut-outs on the sheet, the
flooring rows and their room lines on the NET figure with the deductions
in words, and a Deduct click that toggles a listed object or asks the
engine for any other enclosed shape with every line type as wall.
Measured on Church A2.1: MAIN BATH tub 9.2 SF (4.7 × 2.2, the sheet's
TSEA63R), PRIMARY BATH tub 10.8 SF (2.8 × 4.9, the sheet's MAAX 36 × 60
unit), both out; kitchen island 8.2 SF out by one click.
`out/verify/2026-09-09/fixtures/VERIFY.md`.

STILL OPEN. Cell sizes run a line width under catalogue sizes; the gates
were widened for it. A fixture with no line on its wall side does not
enclose a cell and needs the click. Sheets with no layer names list
shapes but deduct nothing on their own.

TRAP for the next session: patch files through the Edit tool or in binary
mode. `open(p).read()` / `write()` on Windows converts LF to CRLF, which
breaks every multi-line regex in tests/*.js and moves the earthwork
signature for a whitespace change.

## The point editor and the 3D view (dev, not on prod)

Koby's next ask after the fixtures: "add points anywhere upon a line to
easily manipulate its shape for things we won't be able to auto detect
properly", and "3D modeling/rendering of the layouts ... common textures
as well as the colors for rooms/areas". Built, in webui/app.js and a new
webui/fl3d.js:

EDIT SHAPE (tool `fl-edit`, in POINT_TOOLS / FL_TOOLS, a mode button and
a per-room `edit` button): click a room to select it; every corner gets a
handle; drag a corner; press on an edge to insert a corner at the
projection and drag it in one gesture; double-click or Delete removes a
corner (never below 3). Area and perimeter are recomputed from the polygon
(`flEditRecompute`), the door openings / ceiling / fixtures stay as
picked, `source` becomes "edited" (persisted; the row says "shape edited
by hand"), and the takeoff rows re-apply. The mouse handlers run in the
CAPTURE phase on the window and stop propagation, so the sheet's pan and
the selected-line vertex drag never see a press the editor took; a press
off the outline falls through (pan, or the click that selects another
room). Measured live on Church A2.1 BED #2 with real mouse gestures:
136.4 → 144.1 SF after one corner drag, → 153.7 with an added corner,
back to 144.1 after removing it; survived a reload.
`out/verify/2026-09-09/point-editor-3d/VERIFY.md`.

3D VIEW (button in the flooring panel): webui/fl3d.js and the vendored
three.js r147 + OrbitControls (webui/vendor, MIT) load on the first press.
Each room is a floor slab (THREE.Shape from its polygon in feet, the
deducted fixtures and clicked-out shapes as holes) in a procedural
texture chosen by the floor type's NAME - carpet / tile / wood / LVP /
plain - tinted with the panel's colour, repeat in feet; translucent wall
slabs per outline edge to the room's ceiling (its note, else the panel
default, else the bar's number, else 9 ft); a screen-sized label per
room; a drafting grid; orbit / zoom / pan. Switches: textures, walls,
default wall height. It draws the takeoff's numbers and changes none.

STILL OPEN. Editing does not move a door bridge or its opening deduction
(the door count stays as picked); a corner dragged across the ring makes
a self-intersecting polygon and the area is the signed sum with no
warning. The 3D textures are generic by name - a type named "Type 3"
renders plain; there is no per-type material picker yet, and no doors or
windows in the walls (the bridges are known; cutting them is the next
step). Light theme rendered only by reasoning (background and grid
colours), not by eye.
