## F63 — Spot grades: 225 printed elevations the engine could not see (3d431c4)
Found by working Beaumont's job as their estimator (out/verify/2026-08-21/operator/OPERATOR.md).
Grading sheets abbreviate: "FG: 15.96" beside contour "7715" means 7715.96. The reader's
`\d{3,4}(\.\d{1,2})?` never matched a two-digit value, so ALL of them were invisible - 125 on
C3.1, 55 on C3.2, 45 on C3.4. `read_spot_grades()` pairs TAG:value and resolves the hundreds by
CONSENSUS (a real contour label's own last two digits must sit inside the spot-value spread, and
two labels must agree) - this is what rejects the sheet DATE (2026) and the legend's 7900, both
of which produced garbage elevations in the first cut. Surface gains .points so a TIN can be
built from spot grades alone. C3.4 45/45 resolved 7712.00-7731.17; C3.1 125 found, 0 resolved
(no usable label -> the UI asks for a datum; we never invent one).
