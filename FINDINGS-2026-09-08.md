## 1. Dimension-string scale check — `takeoff_engine/dimcheck.py`

WHAT CHANGED. Every printed dimension (`20'-6"`, `44' - 0"`, `8"`, stacked
fractions delivered as half-size glyphs) is paired with its dimension line
and the two crossings it is centred between - the line's own ends on an
AutoCAD-style sheet (one line per dimension), the ticks it sits between on
a chained line (Belle) - and divided: printed feet over measured span. The
readings cluster at 4%; a scale is STATED only when 3+ agree and they are
60%+ of everything measured, else the clusters stay visible. Detect writes
`result.dimension_check`; `POST /api/job/{id}/page/{p}/dimcheck` computes it
for an older result. The UI compares against the scale in force right now
(a pick re-runs it): header chip (green agree / red contradiction with the
gap / amber offered), the confidence banner, a "use" button that adopts the
reading through the typed-scale path flagged `from_dimensions`, and a canvas
overlay of every measured span. The export carries `dimension_check`, the
report prints the sentence under the scale statement.

WHAT IT FIXED. Belle A1.00: caption 4 ft/in, drawn bar agrees, all 7 printed
dimensions measure 8.04 - every flooring square foot on that sheet was 4x
low with two green readings above it. The "confirmed two ways" line goes
amber when the dimensions refute it: a bar drawn beside its caption is one
reading, not two.

MEASURED. Belle 7 of 7 at 8.04 (after preferring oblique tick crossings over
the dashed grid lines that sat inside them); Church A2.1 110 of 112 at 4.00
(107 in the live detect); A1.20 door/window types 72 of 84 at 4.10 - that
sheet prints at 97.6%.

STILL OPEN. A sheet with a plan and details at two scales abstains ("split")
and lists both clusters; it does not yet say which region each governs
beyond the cluster centroid. Dimension text pushed outside a tiny span is
skipped. Scanned sheets have no text layer and get no check.

## 2. Ceiling heights -> wall area

`rooms.ceiling_note` reads the height written in the room ("9'-1 1/8"
CEILING", "CLG. HT. 10'-0"", stacked on the line above/below the ceiling
word when the label is narrow); VAULTED reports no height. The panel: a
per-room height box prefilled from the sheet (dashed border), a default for
rooms with no note, and a `Wall area — perimeter × ceiling` SF row with a
line per room naming the basis; door openings out at 6'-8" (a general
practice default, said so). Church: 6 of 10 rooms carry 9'-1 1/8" on the
sheet; W.I.C. lost its name and its ceiling on the first pass (no
three-letter run in "W.I.C."; three-line label) - both fixed.

## 3. Wall footage by wall type

`flWallTypes`: the wall line types classed by CAD layer / legend name
(exterior / interior / shear / unclassified; hatch and flooring boundaries
are "not a wall"), line LF per class, the face count MEASURED (share of
segments with a parallel partner 2-14 pt away) and the centreline
estimate; "Label wall lines by type" relabels the measured rows (`Wall —
interior`, `Wall — exterior`), centreline rows go in only when ticked.
Church: exterior 133 LF single line, interior 1,463 LF of line / 731 LF of
wall at 2 faces (81% paired). Belle has no layers: everything is
"wall (unclassified)" plus the legend's SHEAR WALL.

## 4. Per-room lines in the report

`extra_items[].details` -> approve keeps them (bounded), the report prints
`- BED #2: 136.4 SF` under each flooring / base / waste / wall-area /
transition row, the CSV writes `part: <room>` rows tagged `part of <row>`,

times, minus, arrow drew as boxes in the base font).

## 5. Multi-leaf door runs

`rooms._split_arcs`: a chain cut into straight pieces (leaves, stubs) and
curved pieces (split again at a >40 degree cusp), each arc fitted alone; a
door needs its leaf, a partner arc one diameter away, or a named door
layer; two or more arcs beat the single fit (which passed one big circle
through a synthetic pair). Church: 7 doors from the 4 multi-arc chains the
single fit rejected. Two more fixes the bed 2 closet forced: the leaf pass
no longer makes a second door out of a stroke whose far end is a known
hinge, and `door_bridges` takes the bearing WITHOUT drawn leaf ink when the
other bearing has it (the ray behind the hinge along the open leaf ran
through wall hatch and outscored the real opening).
