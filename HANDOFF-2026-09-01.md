## Known-bad things that are NOT yet fixed

1. **`takeoff-scaleui` tells the operator a falsehood.** Its paragraph says
   *"nothing else on this sheet — no drawn scale bar — backs them up"* on a
   sheet that visibly prints a checkered 0/40'/80' bar directly above the
   caption. The engine failed to *measure* the bar; the sentence converts that
   into "the drawing doesn't have one" — and on that sheet the bar is the one
   printed thing separating 40 ft/in from 300 ft/in, a factor of 7.5. It
   argues the operator out of the only check available. The button's own
   tooltip gets it right (*"Nothing on this sheet has been checked against
   it"* — a claim about the engine, not the drawing); borrow that wording.
   Same paragraph asserts a cause the code never tests ("because this sheet's
   text is drawn as shapes") — measured false: 655 characters, 130 words, five
   embedded fonts.
2. **Owner-less jobs are writable by every account** (pre-existing, not from
   any branch). Delete and reset refuse; pricing, approvals, earthwork and
   structure writes do not. On the production box that means genuine
   pre-login client uploads are readable and writable by any account. Needs a
   decision, separately from any merge.
3. **Six annotation layers, 108,743 pt, are still counted** by Defect 0's
   matcher — deliberately, because `BORDER` might be a planting-bed edge and
   the corpus contains `BG-L-Metal edger`. Recorded as a miss, not defended.
