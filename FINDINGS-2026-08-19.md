## Findings (ranked, each verified before listing)

### F1 — Scale OCR misses value-before-keyword layouts  [REAL, fix queued]
C4.1–C4.4 detailed grading plans carry `SCALE: 1" = 10'` as exploded text.
OCR reads it, but token order arrives as `1" 10' SCALE:` (value before
keyword) and the SCALE-anchored pattern only looks after the keyword.
Likely also behind several other "no scale" plan pages.

### F2 — GCHC stair plans: spike storms (61–69/page)  [REAL, investigate]
A brand-new document family. Stair treads = dense short parallel lines;
something in chaining welds them with backtracks. Worst three pages of the
whole sweep by an order of magnitude. Needs overlay render + diagnosis
before any code.

### F3 — No scale on several real plan sets  [TRIAGE each]
47/73 vector+hybrid pages report no scale, but most are legitimately
scale-less (details, legends, notes, spec manual, price sheets, cover).
Real plan pages to triage: GCHC (all), Sheridan p1–4, CO3 Rifle p2–4,
Plan Set p2–4, Oncor p1–2. GCHC has no scale text even in OCR — check for
graphic bar / title-block scale by eye.

### F4 — Low captured-ink is mostly correct rejection  [VERIFIED, no fix]
Pages at 9–41% captured ink are texture/photo/notes-heavy: Oncor p2(idx1)
= notes sheet whose 10k small straight segments are table rules (5%
glyph-like, correctly unchained); RC Ontario = aerial-photo hybrids;
C10.x/C11.0 = detail sheets (hatch). The metric flags them; the eye clears
them. EXCEPTION to re-check: C4.2 NE grading at 46% — a real plan page.

### F5 — Legend reading is narrow but not wrong
Rows found on 8 pages, and zero pages with rows-found-but-none-applied.
Most sheets rely on the dedicated legend sheet (C1.1/C1.2, GCHC Legend) —
cross-sheet legend application is the known open feature, unchanged.
